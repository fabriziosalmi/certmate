{# The dashboard's own controls, living in the global topbar (#359). These used to be a band at the top of the page content: the view toggle on the left, the certificate counters on the right, and an empty row between them and the table. Moving them into the topbar frees that whole row on the one page where vertical space is worth the most. The topbar is shared by every page, so this renders only on `/` — the toggle and the counters are about certificates, and there is nothing for them to toggle anywhere else. `x-data` makes this an Alpine root, and the state it reads is the `certs` store registered at the top of this file — because the control that writes the view and the panels that read it are now in two different templates and cannot share an `x-data` scope. The registration ships with the only markup that reads it, so a page cannot end up with one half and not the other. Element ids are unchanged — `#statsCards` is where `updateStats()` writes, `#totalCount`/`#activeCount`/`#revokedCount` are where `ccLoadStatistics()` writes, and the command palette clicks `#certViewServerBtn` / `#certViewClientBtn` to change view. Only the surrounding layout moved. From `xl` (1280px) the cluster sits in the bar, between the 64px logo and the 443px icon rail, and gets what is left: 698px at 1280, 709px from about 1296 up (the `max-w-7xl` container stops growing there), 674 to 685px inside its padding. In it: the 367px toggle, a 12px gap, and the counter strip, 245px with two-digit counts and 280px with three (measured 2026-11-16). It must never wrap there. It did, from the ACME button (#1423) on: a wrap makes the bar 108px tall instead of 65, and since the strip's width follows the counts, the bar changed height while the dashboard loaded, when the placeholder became numbers. So the cluster is `xl:flex-nowrap`, and when four-digit counts do not fit the strip's labels shorten with an ellipsis (`truncate`); the numbers and the bar's height do not change. tests/test_ui_nothing_on_screen_moves_while_a_page_loads.py holds this. #}
